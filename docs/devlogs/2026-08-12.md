## Stop carving the pending block out of rcheevos' arena

*2026-08-12 01:08*

Isolated on hardware, one change at a time. Reverting both of this branch's
changes to arm9_ra brought achievements back -- two stages, two real ids,
notifications where they belong. Restoring only the arena's shortened top
took them away again: 0xF0000000 out of the unlock ring seconds into the
game, and nothing real triggering after it. The per-tick store is exonerated.

So 512 bytes off the top of that heap breaks the binary. The fit test
reported 11 KB of margin at the time, so scarcity is not the explanation and
the real one is still unknown -- something is using an address the allocator
was previously allowed to reach, or reaching past the top is load-bearing in
a way nothing here documents.

The fix does not depend on knowing which. The arena goes back exactly where
it was and nothing is carved out of it: the pending tally moves into the top
of the definitions' own 32 KB reservation, which is already excluded from the
heap and of which the largest real set measured uses 8,306. The launcher's
cap comes down by the same 512 so a set can never grow into it.

The comment on the arena now says not to shorten it and why, since the reason
is a hardware measurement that cannot be recovered from the code.

Two other things this run settled.

The `ndsHeader` reading is confirmed unsafe, by the pairing rather than by
argument: this build's unlock fired seconds into the game and carries YCTE
and CONTRA 4, while the previous build's fired at stage ends, minutes in, and
carried neither. The header at 0x027FFE00 is the loader's, and the game reuses
that memory once it is running. Capturing both fields once at cardengine init
is the fix; not done here, since this commit is the isolation's conclusion and
should stay one change.

And ra_fit_test.c has now been wrong in both directions. It followed the
pending block, which was 512 bytes below the real top -- conservative -- and
then 32 KB above it once the block moved, which is the dangerous direction for
a check that exists to ask whether the set still fits. It follows the
definitions block, which is what ra_startup() is actually passed.

Suite passes with 0 failures; arena 140,852 bytes with 12,500 spare. Builds
clean at RA_LAUNCHER_WIFI=0 and =2.

## Put the pending block back where the working build had it

*2026-08-12 01:21*

Two lines, and they undo the wrong conclusion of the commit before this one.

The diff settles what the isolation could not: arm9_ra is code-identical
between the build where achievements fire and the one where 0xF0000000 comes
out of the unlock ring -- only comments differ. So the fault was never in
that binary and never in the arena. What differs is where the bootloader
copies these 512 bytes.

It worked at DEFS_LOCATION - 0x200 and broke at the top of the window,
0x0377FE00. That is the opposite of any corruption story I had: in the build
that worked the block sat *inside* the heap, where the allocator could reach
it, and in both that failed it sat outside.

So the address goes back, and nothing else changes. What the last 512 bytes
of that 256K window are for is now the open question, and it is a real one --
something is there that the definitions' own reservation apparently does not
cover.

Accepted for now, and it will show: the block is inside the arena again, so
the allocator can hand out the memory the menu reads and Sync Pending may
show nonsense. The game behaving correctly comes first.

arm9_ra verified identical to 4c565ee by diff. Suite passes with 0 failures;
arena 140,852 bytes. Builds clean at RA_LAUNCHER_WIFI=0 and =2.

## Publish what the definitions block actually contains

*2026-08-12 03:00*

Six builds were spent inferring why achievements stopped firing, and every
one of them was reasoning about code instead of reading the machine. The
snapshot could say what happened after the staged definitions arrive and
nothing at all about whether they did.

Two readings are certain and cannot both describe a working copy: the
launcher's log reaches stage 15 of 15 and reports staging 45 definitions,
and the game reports rcFromFile 0 with one built-in definition and the
synthetic id 0xF0000000. So the block is lost between them. Inspection says
the bootloader's copy is correct, the staging regions are disjoint, the call
order is right and the bootloader is rebuilt -- all true, and none of it
found the fault.

So the first word and the length at CARDENGINEI_ARM9_RA_DEFS_LOCATION are
published raw, before anything is decided from them:

  'RDA1' with a bad length  -- copied, header wrong
  0                         -- the bootloader took its else branch: it looked,
                               found nothing staged, and cleared the destination
  anything else             -- nothing was copied and this is what the window held

That third case is the one no amount of reading the copy could find, because
the copy is correct: it would mean the branch containing it never ran.

The snapshot grows from 0xC4 to 0xCC; the ARM9 cardengine has 1,216 bytes
left. The two new offsets are pinned by the host suite like the rest.

Suite passes with 0 failures. Builds clean at RA_LAUNCHER_WIFI=0 and =2.
